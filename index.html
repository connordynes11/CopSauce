Our Founder's Journey

Our founder was always the most progressive guy in the room. He recycled before it was cool, corrected everyone's terminology, and rocked bright blue hair with pride. And for that, he got bullied. A lot.

One night, after yet another day of getting picked on for being super liberal, he wandered into his kitchen looking for comfort. He found peppers. He found garlic. He found a tub of protein powder. And he made the first batch of CopSauce.

He put it on everything. He hit the gym. The gains came fast. Before long, the blue-haired kid everyone picked on was the most jacked man in town, and also the most respectful.

Today he shares CopSauce with the world so nobody ever has to choose between their values and their biceps.
